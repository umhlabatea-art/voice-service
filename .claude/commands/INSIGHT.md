# /INSIGHT — Strategic Intelligence Briefing

**Usage**: `/INSIGHT [scope]`  
**Scope options**: `market` · `competitor` · `product` · `expansion` · `pricing`

---

## Scope Definitions

### market
SA OHS compliance SaaS landscape:
- CIDB contractor market size (Grade 1–9)
- Events industry licence holder volume
- Current manual compliance workflow pain points
- Digital adoption rate among target contractors

### competitor
Top 3 competitor deep analysis:
```
For each competitor:
  - Company name, product, pricing
  - Strengths (what they do well)
  - Weaknesses (exploitable gaps)
  - Our advantage over them
  - Leverage strategy
```

### product
Feature gap analysis vs market leaders:
- Missing features in Umsavati OHS vs competitors
- Highest-demand features not yet built
- Quick wins (build in < 1 sprint)
- Strategic differentiators to double down on

### expansion
Global vertical entry assessment:
Hypothesis: UK/EU · Gulf · Australia
- Regulatory landscape per region
- OHS framework equivalents to SA OHS Act
- Market entry complexity (1–10 scale)
- Revenue potential vs cost to serve
- Recommended priority order

### pricing
Hormozi value-stack optimisation:
- Current offer audit
- Value stack reconstruction (what's actually worth $$$)
- Irresistible offer architecture
- Price anchoring strategy
- Upsell/cross-sell map (SaaS → SafeFile Pro™ → Consulting)
